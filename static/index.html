Stock Signal

jo measure hua, wahi dikhta hai
Company ka naam likho — signal yahin aa jayega.

Forecast card ek logistic-regression model hai jo har baar pichhle data par train hota hai aur walk-forward me test hota hai. Jab tak uska out-of-sample record baseline se behtar nahi, wo "NO TRADE" hi bolega — chahe probability bar kitna bhi bada dikhe.

Har signal ke saath uska aakhri 40% data ka nateeja dikhta hai — cost aur slippage kaat kar, saath me confidence interval. Agar wo number laal hai, us signal par paisa mat lagao, chahe direction kitna bhi convincing lage.
